Chest radiograph • Educational utility

CXR report-to-anatomy explorer

Map terms in a chest X-ray report onto approximate, predefined anatomical regions.

Not a diagnostic system. This tool does not analyze pixels, identify pathology, or validate radiological findings. Boxes are fixed anatomical templates, not model predictions. Use de-identified material only.

1. Study and report

PNG, JPEG or WebP; maximum 20 MB. The image stays in this browser. DICOM files are not supported here.

Up to 100 lines, 400 characters per line. For demonstration, use upright PA/AP radiographs; templates are not aligned to patient anatomy.

Ready. All processing takes place locally.
